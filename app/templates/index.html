Zero-Disk Privacy Model

AntiTelguard enforces strict volatile data retention policies. When deployed in community or enterprise environments, chat participants' files are protected by design:

IN_MEMORY_STREAM

Incoming file chunks (64KB) are pumped directly into hashlib.sha256() in RAM. Zero bytes are written to persistent storage or /tmp.

NO_FILE_UPLOADS

Only the 64-character SHA-256 digest is transmitted to VirusTotal. Unrecognized files are never submitted to public feeds without explicit instruction.

SELF_SOVEREIGN

Hosted entirely on your own container infrastructure. Telemetry, tokens, and audit trails remain isolated inside your local data/ volume.
